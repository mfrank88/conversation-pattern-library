Grace 2.0 Flow Library
Purpose: one working library for Grace voice flows, shared rules, SMS copy and open questions.
Status: corrected draft for review. Existing flow structure is preserved: summary, dialogue standards, steps, decisions, sample dialogue, end states and divider lines.
Flow Index
Use the document outline to jump between sections. Each flow keeps the same step-and-decision format as the source flows.


Active Flows
* Verify Appointment: helps a caller check upcoming booked appointment details and request a text with appointment details.
* Cancel Appointment: helps a caller cancel an upcoming appointment, offers reschedule first when the appointment is in scheduling scope and confirms cancellation.
* Schedule Appointment: helps a caller book a new appointment for themselves, including triage, slot search, lock and confirmation.
* Reschedule Appointment: helps a caller move an existing appointment, including entry from Cancel with selected appointment context.


Shared Components
* Shared Rules: openings, authentication, proxy callers, global escalation, survey logic, transfer logic and end states.
* SMS Spec: message templates, character rules, fallback rules and send failure handling.
* Open Items: items that should stay visible until compliance, product or engineering confirms them.


Cross-Flow Routing
* Verify can route to Schedule when no upcoming appointment exists and the caller wants to book.
* Cancel can route to Reschedule after the appointment is selected and confirmed, if the appointment is in Phase 1 scheduling scope.
* Schedule, Reschedule, Cancel and Verify can route to one another after the closing check if the caller has another task.
* Any flow can route to Care Navigator through DECISION L when out of scope, unclear after allowed retry, after hours, system failure, proxy caller in V1 or caller asks for a person.
* Schedule and Reschedule can route to EMERGENCY END when Dash triage returns an emergency response. Wording is pending compliance review.
SHARED RULES
These rules apply across all Grace voice flows unless a flow states a tighter rule.


Voice And Tone
* Grace does not use first-person pronouns in conversation: no "I," "me" or "my."
* The opening line is the one allowed exception because Grace identifies itself by name.
* Avoid "I understand," "I'm sorry," "I hope," feeling claims and medical advice.
* Keep language around seventh-grade reading level.
* Lead with what Grace can do.
* Use "you" and "your" when speaking to the caller.


Standard Openings


No sub-intent from Genesys:
"This is Grace, an AI-powered assistant ready to help with scheduling, rescheduling, canceling and verifying appointments. What do you need to do today?"


Verify intent captured:
"This is Grace, an AI-powered assistant. Ready to check on that appointment, or were you calling about something else?"


Cancel intent captured:
"This is Grace, an AI-powered assistant. Ready to help cancel an appointment, or was the call about something else?"


Schedule intent captured:
"This is Grace, an AI-powered assistant. Ready to help with that new appointment, or was the call about something else?"


Reschedule intent captured:
"This is Grace, an AI-powered assistant. Ready to help reschedule that appointment, or was the call about something else?"


Authentication
Primary authentication prompt:
"To pull up your record, what's your name and date of birth?"


* Authentication: name and DOB collected in one turn. DOB accepted via speech or DTMF (keypad). DTMF supports 6-digit (MMDDYY) or 8-digit (MMDDYYYY) entry. No readback on first attempt. Two retries allowed. First retry reads back captured values (no match) or re-explains input options (wrong format) before re-asking. Second retry states the information isn't coming up and re-asks for date of birth only, with both input modes offered. Three failed attempts total escalates to a Care Navigator.
* Multi-patient phone scenarios are out of V1 self-service scope. Go to DECISION L.


Proxy Caller Or Patient Switch
V1 rule:
* If the caller says the call is for another patient at session start, go to DECISION L.
* If the caller switches to another patient after authentication, exit the current step and trigger the On Behalf Of flow once it ships.
* Until that flow ships, transfer to a Care Navigator.
* Carry forward only call metadata for the new patient subject. Do not carry forward the original patient context.


Sample dialogue:
"Calls for another patient need a Care Navigator. Connecting you now."


Global Escalation
At any point, if the caller asks for a person using the approved trigger set, route immediately to DECISION L.


Approved trigger set for V1:
* Agent
* Operator
* Help


Also route to DECISION L for distress, repeated frustration, unclear intent after allowed retry, unsupported request, system error or workflow dead end.


Closing Check - Standard


"Is there anything else needed today?"


Use the same closing loop across flows:
* If yes, capture the next intent and route to the matching flow.
* After that task completes, return to the same flow's closing check.
* If no, run survey eligibility.


Survey Eligibility
Survey only fires when every task in the session completed cleanly, with no human handoff and no out-of-scope intent.


Recommended session flags:
* completed_task: true after a successful verify, cancellation, booking or reschedule.
* incomplete_task: true after an abandoned task or unsupported continuation.
* out_of_scope: true after an unsupported or redirected intent.


Survey-eligible closing:
"Thank you for choosing Ascension, [first name]. To help improve this experience, please share your thoughts in a brief survey. Connecting you now."


Non-survey closing:
"Thank you for choosing Ascension, [first name]."


Transfer And After-Hours End States
DECISION L - Transfer to Care Navigator
Check if within open hours.
* If yes, go to ESCALATE-LIVE END.
* If no, go to ESCALATE-AFTERHOURS END.


ESCALATE-LIVE END:
Sample dialogue:
"A Care Navigator can help with this. Connecting you now."


Pass session context so the Care Navigator does not start over. Include intent, authentication state, selected appointment, appointments returned, slot history, preferences captured, last action taken and triage state when present.


ESCALATE-AFTERHOURS END:
Primary sample dialogue:
"To finish this, please call back during normal business hours when a Care Navigator can help. Thank you for choosing Ascension."


Alternate sample dialogue:
"This needs help from a Care Navigator. They're available during normal business hours, so please call back at that time. Thank you for choosing Ascension."


Emergency Handling
Schedule and Reschedule use Dash triage.
* If Dash returns an emergency response, go to EMERGENCY END.
* Offer emergency care as an option, not a directive.
* Confirm the caller is safe to continue before any further scheduling action.
* Final wording is pending compliance review.
VERIFY APPOINTMENT FLOW


SUMMARY
* Grace opens the call.
* Grace collects name and date of birth and authenticates the caller in one turn.
* Use shared authentication, including speech and keypad DOB.
* Genesys may or may not pass a captured intent to Grace. STEP 1 branches on that signal.
* Verify is read-only: no booking, locking or modification.
* SMS is the only outbound action.
* Dash returns upcoming booked appointments. Behavior branches on appointment count: 0, 1 or 2+.
* For 2 or 3 appointments, Grace reads each date and asks which the caller wants.
* For 4 or more, Grace steps through the dates one at a time.
* Proxy or "on behalf of" scenarios transfer in V1 until the On Behalf Of flow ships.
* SMS confirmation includes both provider profile link and facility map link for every caller.
* SMS send failure uses one retry, then verbal fallback.
* Closing check, survey eligibility and transfer logic use shared rules.

STEP 2 - Authentication
Sample dialogue: "To pull up your record, what's your name and date of birth?"
DECISION 1 - Matches a single patient record?
If Yes (single match), go to STEP 3.
If No match, go to STEP 2B (readback retry).
If wrong format (DTMF entry doesn't parse as a valid date in either format), go to STEP 2B (format retry).
If multi-patient phone, go to DECISION L. Per V1 scope, multi-patient phones transfer.
STEP 2B - First authentication retry (attempt 2)
Grace reads back what was captured (no-match case) or re-explains the input options (wrong-format case) before re-asking.
For no-match: "That didn't match a record. The name was [captured name] and the date was [captured date]. Want to try again?"
For wrong format: "That date didn't come through. Try again. Say the date, or enter 6 digits like 022890, or 8 digits like 02281990."
If the retry matches a single record, go to STEP 3. If it fails again (no match or invalid format), go to STEP 2C.
STEP 2C - Second authentication retry (attempt 3)
Sample dialogue: "Your information isn't coming up. Let's try one more time. You can speak or enter your date of birth on the keypad."
This attempt re-collects date of birth only. The captured name from the prior attempt is held. No readback. Both speech and DTMF remain accepted. If the attempt matches a single record, go to STEP 3. If it fails, go to DECISION L. No fourth attempt under any condition.
STEP 3 - Pull Upcoming Appointments
Retrieve upcoming booked appointments via Dash.
Sample dialogue: "One moment while the appointments come up."


DECISION A - How many upcoming appointments?
* Zero, go to STEP 4A.
* One, go to STEP 4B.
* Two or more, go to STEP 4C.
______________
STEP 4 - Appointment Selection


STEP 4A - No Upcoming Appointments
Sample dialogue: "There aren't any upcoming appointments on file. Want to schedule one?"
* If yes, route to Schedule flow.
* If no, go to STEP 7.


STEP 4B - Single Appointment
Sample dialogue: "There is one upcoming appointment: [visit type] with Dr. [provider name] at [Facility] on [Day, Date] at [Time]."
Go to STEP 5.


STEP 4C - Multiple Appointments
For N=2:
"There are two upcoming appointments. The first is on [Date] and the second is on [Date]. Do you want details on a specific one or both?"


For N=3:
"There are three upcoming appointments. The first is on [Date], the second is on [Date] and the third is on [Date]. Do you want details on a specific one or all of them?"


For N=4 or more:
"There are [N] upcoming appointments. Let's go through them one at a time. The first is on [Date]. Do you want details on this appointment, the next appointment or all appointments?"


STEP 4C-i - Step-Through Loop
Read the next date in the list.
Sample dialogue: "The next one is on [Date]. Do you want details on this appointment, the next appointment or all appointments?"
Go to DECISION B.


End of list:
If the caller said "next appointment" and that was the last date in the list:
"That was the last one. Want details on a specific one or all of them?"
Go to DECISION B.


DECISION B - Caller selects an appointment?
* If yes, single appointment, share full details: "That appointment is [visit type] with Dr. [provider name] at [Facility] on [Day, Date] at [Time]." Go to STEP 5.
* If yes, next appointment, go to STEP 4C-i.
* If yes, all of them, share full details for each in sequence. Go to STEP 5.
* If escalation signal, go to DECISION L.
* If unclear, allow one retry. If still unclear, go to DECISION L.

STEP 5 - Offer SMS With Appointment Details
Sample dialogue: "Would you like a text with your appointment details?"


DECISION C - Caller accepts SMS?
* If yes, go to STEP 5A.
* If no, go to STEP 7.


STEP 5A - Confirm Phone Number
Sample dialogue: "Should it go to the number ending in [last 4 digits]?"


DECISION D - Caller confirms the number?
* If yes, go to STEP 6.
* If no and caller gives a different number, capture alternate number, then go to STEP 6.
* If no and caller skips the text, go to STEP 7.

STEP 6 - Send SMS
Trigger SMS to confirmed number using the Verify SMS template. For multiple appointments, send one two-message set per appointment.


DECISION SMS-1 - Send successful?
* If yes, sample dialogue: "Okay, you're all set." Go to STEP 7.
* If no, go to STEP 6B.


STEP 6B - Retry SMS
Retry once. For two-message sends, retry only the failed message.


DECISION SMS-2 - Send successful on retry?
* If yes, go to STEP 7.
* If no, go to STEP 6C.


STEP 6C - Verbal Fallback
Recap verbally only the appointment details whose SMS failed.
Sample dialogue: "The text isn't going through right now. The appointment is on [date] at [time] with [provider_full] at [facility_name]."
Go to STEP 7.

Sample dialogue: "Is there anything else needed today?"


DECISION E - Anything else?
* If yes, go to STEP 7B.
* If no, go to DECISION F.


STEP 7B - Capture Next Intent
Identify the next task and route to the matching flow. After that task is complete, loop back to STEP 7.


DECISION F - Survey Eligibility Check
Survey only fires when every task in the session completed cleanly with no human handoff and no out-of-scope intent.
* If all requests are complete, go to CLOSE-SURVEY END.
* Otherwise, go to CLOSE-NO-SURVEY END.


DECISION L - Transfer to Care Navigator
Check if within open hours.
* If yes, go to ESCALATE-LIVE END.
* If no, go to ESCALATE-AFTERHOURS END.


END

* CLOSE-NO-SURVEY END: Deliver non-survey closing, then end call. No survey.
* ESCALATE-LIVE END: Say "A Care Navigator can help with this. Connecting you now." Transfer with session context. No survey.
* ESCALATE-AFTERHOURS END: Deliver after-hours message. End call. No transfer. No survey.

SUMMARY

* Submit cancellation, then confirm with a spoken caution about related appointments.
* Offer SMS confirmation only. No email.
* SMS send failure uses one retry, then verbal fallback.
* Closing check, survey eligibility and transfer logic use shared rules.

SCHEDULE APPOINTMENT FLOW


SUMMARY
* Grace opens the call. Captured intent from Genesys, if any, determines which open.
* Authentication uses shared authentication with spoken or keypad DOB when enabled.
* Pull last 10 appointments via Dash to identify recurring providers, specialties and visit patterns.
* Three caller entry patterns: reason for visit, provider or specialty.
* If the resolved reason has has_triage true, walk the Dash decision tree.
* Emergency outcomes route to EMERGENCY END.
* Phase 1 launch: Primary Care only. Phase 2 adds Cardiovascular once Phase 1 metrics support expansion.
* Office vs telehealth is filtered silently by Dash eligible-for-telehealth flag.
* Slot search window is 7 days by default. Acute reasons may require booking within 48 hours.
* Slot lock holds for 15 minutes. Release lock if caller backs out before booking.
* SMS branching based on first visit is removed. Every Schedule SMS includes both facility map link and provider profile link.
* Slot-search elimination reason text is internal only. Log it, but do not speak it to callers.
* Closing check, survey eligibility and transfer logic use shared rules.

RESCHEDULE APPOINTMENT FLOW


SUMMARY
* Grace opens the call. Captured intent from Genesys, if any, determines which open.
* Authentication uses shared authentication with spoken or keypad DOB when enabled.
* Pull upcoming appointments from Dash. Window: 90 days, capped at 10.
* If multiple appointments exist, ask which one. Read back chronologically. Branch on count: 0, 1, 2-3, 4-10 or more than 10.
* Two entry paths: standard entry where caller picks appointment in this flow, and entry from Cancel where appointment context is already loaded.
* Scope and eligibility check after appointment selection uses Dash reschedulability flag.
* Date and time are primary levers. Default to same provider, same reason, same location and same telehealth flag.
* If Dash returns triage required, drop into Schedule-style triage with a softening line.
* Slot search window is 7 days by default. Acute reasons may require booking within 48 hours.
* Slot lock holds for 15 minutes. Release lock if caller backs out before submission.
* After two failed preference rounds with same provider, pivot to a care team alternate before escalating.
* Reschedule executes as a single atomic call via Dash. If API fails, original appointment stays in place.
* SMS uses two messages with facility map link and provider profile link for every caller.
* SMS send failure uses one retry, then verbal fallback.
* Closing check, survey eligibility and transfer logic use shared rules.

SMS SPEC
SMS Dialogue Spec - Verify, Cancel, Schedule, Reschedule - corrected library draft


Shared Rules
* 160 characters per individual message.
* Verify, Schedule and Reschedule use two purposeful messages by default when full appointment details are included.
* Cancel uses one message.
* Two-message sends end with (1/2) and (2/2).
* Verify multi-appointment sends use one independent two-message set per appointment.
* Verify, Schedule and Reschedule include Ascension by name in Message 1.
* Cancel identifies Ascension through ascension.org and Ascension One app in the same message.
* Consent and opt-out are handled by Ascension's SMS program.
* No emoji.
* SMS copy is declarative, neutral and confirmational.


Format Conventions
* Date: Wed Dec 31. Abbreviated day, abbreviated month, numeric date, no comma, no year unless appointment is more than a year away.
* Time: 10:20am. 12-hour, lowercase am/pm, no space.
* Provider: [first] [last], [credential]. No Dr. prefix.
* Facility: full name as returned from Dash.
* Address: full street address, no zip.
* City: sentence case, no state.
* Map link: [vanity_map].
* Provider profile link: [vanity_provider].


Long-Content Fallback
For Message 2 in Verify, Schedule or Reschedule, when filled length exceeds 160, drop street address and city. The map URL encodes both.


Send Failure Handling
* Retry once on initial send failure.
* For two-message sends, retry only failed message or messages.
* If retry also fails, Grace gives verbal fallback before closing check.

VERIFY SMS
Single appointment uses two messages.


Message 1 template:
Thank you for calling Ascension to request a one-time message regarding your appointment: [provider_full], [date] at [time]. (1/2)


Message 2 template:
Location: [facility_name], [street_address], [city]. For a map, visit [vanity_map]. For info on your provider, [vanity_provider]. (2/2)


Message 2 fallback:
Location: [facility_name]. For a map, visit [vanity_map]. For info on your provider, [vanity_provider]. (2/2)


Multi-appointment Verify:
One SMS set per appointment, chronological order matching voice readback. Do not number across all messages as (1/4) through (4/4).
